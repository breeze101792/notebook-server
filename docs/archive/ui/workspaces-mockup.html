Workspace picker — reference mockup

Named local notebook roots. One control, two mounts: a chip in the topbar and a glyph on the activity bar, exactly one visible at a time, sharing one popover. Static: nothing here talks to a server. Colours, sizes and radii are the live values from static/css/style.css; workspaces.md is the spec.

1 · Chip in the topbar — picker open

Mount 1. The chip sits after the brand, behind a 1 px divider, and carries the workspace name because the topbar has room for it. The popover hangs below it, left edges aligned, 6 px gap. Rows are navigation, not toggles: aria-current="page" on the active row, the page reloads on pick.

Switching reloads the page.

2 · Glyph on the activity bar — topbar hidden

Mount 2. With hideTopbar the whole topbar is gone, so the chip with it; the same control reappears as an 18 px glyph at the top of the rail, above the view icons. The popover opens to its right, tops aligned. CSS-only: a saved setting paints the right mount on the first frame, with no script.

3 · Mount states

Both mounts, every state that changes their look. The full row-by-row picker states are in board 4.

Chip · long name

Name ellipsised at the 240 px cap. The full name and path are in title.

Chip · active folder missing

Glyph turns --ws-warn and a ⚠ joins the row. The chip stays usable: the popover is the way out.

Rail · default, open, missing (topbar hidden)

No ⚠ on the rail: no room. The glyph colour and the title carry the fault.

Panel collapsed · chip unaffected

The chip is in the topbar, so collapsing the panel does not move it. The rail glyph stays hidden because the topbar is on.

5 · Settings → Workspaces

Its own nav section, not a General subsection: the content is a managed list with per-row actions, it grows with the number of roots, and it is the only place a path is shown in full. Active and Default are separate facts; both can be true.
