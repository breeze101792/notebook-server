Notebook · UI design · lightboxes

Four viewers, one overlay

The shipped full-screen viewer for Mermaid, WaveDrom, Graphviz and SVG — codified, not redesigned. One scrim, one body, one five-item pill: zoom out, percentage, zoom in, fit, close. The chrome does not change with the theme; only the diagram does. Boards are 861 px desktop stages, except the 390 phone. The pill and every value are the shipped ones; the one addition drawn is the focus ring, and it is labelled.

open, at fit or at a zoom level loading and error are not triggers proposals are not drawn here Read with lightboxes.md.

8 · Focus on a control

No focus rule exists today, so a keyboard user cannot see the focused button. Exception E5 adds a 2 px white outline at a 2 px offset: 3.36:1 in the worst case (pill over a white diagram), 21:1 over a black one. It is the only visible pixel this document adds, and it paints on :focus-visible only.

Exception E4 also moves focus to the close button on open, wraps Tab inside the four controls, and returns focus to the opening card on close. No visual change beyond this ring.

Why white and not black: controls.md proposes a black ring. The pill composites to #8c8c8c over a white diagram and to #000 over a black one, so a black ring vanishes on dark drawings. White clears 3:1 at both ends.

9 · Loading and error — not triggers

There is no loading, error or empty state inside the overlay, because those states never produce a trigger. While a renderer runs, the fence is still a fence; when it fails, the warn box is not one of the four container classes. Both are shown here as the precondition, not as a lightbox.

The box has no .mermaid-container class, so the delegated handler finds no container and returns. No overlay, no toast from here.

The overlay is reachable only from a rendered card. That is why it needs no loading state and no error state of its own.
