Secondary sidebar

Two rails, two panels, both open at once. The grid becomes five columns; the Outline becomes a normal view in the right panel. Boards A–J at the shipped numbers, dark and light where it matters.

Illustrative. The normative spec is docs/ui/secondary-sidebar.md; where this mockup and that document differ, the document wins. Tokens are foundations.md Appendix A; frame values are shell-frame.md §1–§5.

1 · The five-column grid

Order left to right: activity rail 48, left panel 240, editor 1fr, right panel 240, right rail 48. The right rail is the far-right edge column; the right panel sits between the editor and that rail. Only the two panels resize, each from its inner edge.

  • Borders. The left panel keeps its right border. The right panel and the right rail each carry a left border, so the seam between editor and right panel is drawn once and the seam between right panel and right rail is drawn once.
  • Indicator mirrors. Left rail: the bar sits on the rail's left edge (left: -6px, the 6 px button gutter). Right rail: the same bar sits on the rail's right edge (right: -6px). Same 2 px --accent, same 4 px top/bottom inset.
  • Headers. One 34 px header per panel, title uppercase --text-xs muted, actions 24 px. The left panel's collapse chevron is nb-icon-chevron-left (toward its rail); the right panel's is nb-icon-chevron-right (toward its rail).
  • Resize edges. Both strips are 8 px, on the editor side — right on the left panel, left on the right panel. Minimum --shell-pane-min 140; right panel default 240.
  • Width tokens. The left panel reads --side-panel-width; the right panel reads --aux-panel-width; both rails read --activity-bar-width. Each panel clamps to min(480, 45 % of the budget), and the budget subtracts both rails and the 352 px reader floor.
  • No tab-bar outline control. The old toggle is gone; the tab list runs to the editor's right edge. The Outline's only rail icon is the one in the right rail.
