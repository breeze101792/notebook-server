Two design windows, codified

The shipped bodies of the Mermaid visual designer and the Graphviz / WaveDrom design window. Geometry, type and colour are the shipped values. The shared header, action bar, footer and focus trap are quoted, not redesigned. The only additions are the seven accessibility and consistency exceptions in diagram-designers.md §8.4.
