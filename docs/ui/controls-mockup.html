44 px targets, 16 px fields, sheets for content. Every value here is under the phone query (max-width: 860px, or a coarse pointer up to 1023 px).

What the board shows. The top bar is 52 px with 44 px controls; the rail is 56 px with four equal targets. The edit bar is 52 px, scrolls sideways, and pins Save and Done at the left. Rows are 44. Fields are 16 px and 44 high. Menu items are 48. The banner's action grows to 44. The sheet is bottom-anchored with a 52 px header and a full-width 44 px primary.

What it does not show. The drawer, the scrim, the keyboard inset and the safe-area padding. Those are the shell's, not the controls'. See docs/archive/design/mobile-shell.md §3.3 and §4.3.

Lightbox. Tap-to-zoom is not offered on the phone in v1 (mobile-shell.md §4.7), so the lightbox has no phone state. It is absent, not disabled.
