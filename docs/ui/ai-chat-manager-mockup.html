AI chat bar and chat manager — mockup

Boards are paired: dark left, light right. Read ai-chat-manager.md; it is normative. panel raised well

1 · Chat bar — 240 and 140

The bar sits between the panel header and the provider row. The title owns the full content width; the path, the flag and the three 24 px actions — New, Manage, Clear — share the second line. At 140 the select shows about fourteen characters and the path clamps — the actions keep their targets, nothing is hidden, and the full strings stay in title. Clear's placement and its states have their own section, §8.

Bar: 8 + 30 select + 2 + 24 meta + 4 + 1 border = 69. The log gives up 69 px (340 → 271 at 560). The select is 30 px tall against the provider select's 36 — recorded as exception X1 in the spec.

2 · Chat bar states

Three facts the bar must never hide: a chat from another file, a file with no chat, and no file at all. The locked row is the fourth state: a turn in flight disables the select, New and Manage, while Clear stays live — it is the abort path (§8) — and the composer carries Stop so the reason is on screen.

The flag is a word plus a glyph, never colour alone (--warn 8.55 / 5.52 on the panel). No-file disables New chat, Clear and the composer. Locked disables the select, New and Manage; Clear stays live because it aborts.

3 · Chat manager — with chats, 240 and 140

The manager replaces the provider row, the log, the attach bar and the composer. The current file's group comes first and carries the word current; other files follow by their newest chat; Unfiled is last. Group heads stick. Each row is a title on the full width, then path, time, count and two 24 px row actions. The active row has the wash, the inset accent bar and primary-coloured meta — never muted, which measures 4.04:1 on the wash in the dark theme.

Manager heights: header 35 + search 52 + footer 73 + panel header 34 = 194 of 560, so the list gets 366. A row is 67 (4 + 17 title + 2 + meta 14 + 2 + actions 24 + 4); a group head is 25. At 140 the row is 100 wide: the title still gets the full width, the path clamps to its ellipsis, and the time, count and both 24 px actions keep their space.

4 · Chat manager — empty

No chats at all. The empty block reuses .ai-empty and centres in the list; the footer keeps New chat and the storage line, so the next action and the budget stay on screen.

With no file open the hint becomes Open a note to start a chat about it. and New chat is disabled — the same block, one string.

5 · Inline rename

Started from the Rename button or F2 on the title button. The field replaces the title, is seeded with the full title and fully selected, and carries the accent border plus the 3 px wash instead of a second focus ring. Enter or blur commits; Esc restores the old title and returns focus to the control that started it.

The rename field is 24 px tall, so the row grows by about 7 px while it is open. A commit with only spaces is a cancel: the previous title returns.

6 · Delete — two steps

The first press on Delete replaces the row's meta line with the question and two buttons. Cancel carries focus, because the second press is the destructive one. Esc or Cancel restores the row and returns focus to the row's Delete button; nothing is removed until Delete is pressed. The title stays readable above the question.

At 140 the question takes one line and the two buttons fit under it; the block wraps rather than clipping. The title above the question is what the user is confirming, so both stay visible.

7 · Storage — over the 4 MB budget

The storage line is always present. Over the budget it keeps its own numbers and a warning appears beneath it: raised face, --warn border, warning glyph, primary text, role="status". Colour is never the only signal, and the warning never replaces the line it is about.

Footer heights: 73 without the warning, ≈140 at 240 and ≈220 at 140 with it, because the sentence wraps to three and nine lines. The list takes the difference; no control moves. The warning's border and glyph carry the state, and the text is --text-primary on --surface-raised (11.30 / 13.48).

8 · Clear moves into the chat bar

Clear used to be a text button in the panel header, next to a chat selector it did not belong to. It now sits in the bar's action group, last, as a 24 px broom: one chat deep, live while the bar is otherwise locked, disabled when there is nothing to clear. The header drops to title plus collapse. The pairs below are the same slice of the view, before and after, so the two headers compare directly.

Clear's box is --control-h-sm 24 and its glyph is --icon-md 16, so the bar's height does not change: the group is 76 px inside the same 24 px line. The one exception is the flag state at 140 px — the last pair — where path + flag + 76 px cannot share a line, the group wraps to a second right-aligned line, and the bar measures 95. Every other state keeps 69.

Before: the header carries Clear and the bar carries two actions. After: the header carries one control and the bar carries three. The shipped header button is drawn with a mockup-only .legacy-clear class so the "before" boards show the bordered face that ships today; the app deletes that rule with the move.
