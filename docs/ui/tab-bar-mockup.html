File tab strip — #tab-bar

Reference rendering for docs/ui/tab-bar.md. Tokens are foundations.md §3–§8; the controls are controls.md §2.8. Every board is drawn dark and light from the same declarations, so any difference between the two is colour only.

Hover and focus are forced with .demo-hover and .demo-focus, because a still cannot be hovered. Pinned widths are pinned inline on the boards that quote a measured number.

1 · Bar states — zero, one, and only pinned

The bar is always 34 px with its bottom hairline. With nothing pinned, #tab-pinned carries hidden and takes no gap. A single tab grows to the 220 rail and the rest of the strip stays empty.

2 · Five mixed tabs

166.4 px each. The active tab joins the page with an accent border; a dirty tab keeps the wash, the dot and a plain border; a conflicting tab leads with the ↻ sprite in --warn. The long name ellipsises, and its state marks survive because they sit outside the label.

3 · Pinned region — three pinned, three unpinned

Pinned tabs rest at max(120, content): 120 / 138 / 164, no close button, a 14 px pin. The region is 426 px, under the 436 cap. The strip keeps 412 and its three tabs are 136 each. No separator between the regions — the bar's own 2 px gap is the spacing.

4 · Overflow — thirteen tabs

Past the 120 px rail the strip stops shrinking and scrolls: 13 × 120 + 12 × 2 = 1584 inside 840. The ninth tab is cut at the clip edge, and that is the whole signal — no scrollbar, no fade, no chevron. A wheel is mapped onto scrollLeft; activating a tab is the only thing that reveals it.

5 · Tab anatomy — the five slots

Left to right: pin 14, conflict 14, the dirty dot 8 (inside the label's start), the label (flexes, ellipsises), close 24 × 24. Gaps are 4. The slot widths under the tab are real; the boxes are 22 high for legibility. All five slots are forced onto one tab so they can be measured — a real pinned tab carries no close button (board 3).

6 · States side by side

Rest, hover, active, dirty, dirty + active, conflicting, pinned, focus. A pinned + conflicting + dirty tab is legal: the name carries all three phrases. Special tabs (italic label, module icon) are not drawn — their icon channel is out of this pass.

7 · Drag — the dragged tab and both drop indicators

The dragged tab is at --opacity-drag (.4). The indicator is an inset 2 px accent stripe, so it cannot be clipped by the region's vertical overflow and does not need the 2 px gap. Top row: drop before. Bottom row: drop after.

8 · Ghost mid-close

A close with the pointer inside the bar leaves an inert copy of the tab's box: flex-basis 202 → 65, opacity 1 → 0.5, at 40 % of the 100 ms collapse. The survivors keep their frozen 202 px and have slid 137 px left. The ghost carries the label only — no pin, no dot, no conflict, no close.

9 · The tab menu

Fourteen items in four groups, as shipped. Move to session is sessions.md's item; the rest is the tab's. Item height 32, box radius 8, --surface-raised, --shadow-pop, clamped to the viewport. "Close to the left" is disabled here because the tab is the first unpinned one.

10 · 861 px viewport — the last desktop pixel

The query is max-width: 860px, so at exactly 860 the phone shell applies and the bar is hidden. 861 is the last desktop pixel. The pane is 861 − 48 − 240 − 220 = 353; the strip is 313, so two tabs at 155.5 fit and a third clamps to 120 and overflows. This is the width where the strip starts to scroll in ordinary use.

11 · 390 px — the bar is not there

#tab-bar { display: none } under the mode switch. The current file is the top-bar title, a 44 px button that opens the drawer; ⋯ closes the file. No strip, no swipe between tabs, no chip row.
