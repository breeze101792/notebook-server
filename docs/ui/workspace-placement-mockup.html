Trade-offs at a glance

The same three facts per board, in one place. Nothing here is hidden by the recommendation. Row cost is the permanent space the control takes in the chrome, not the popover.

Option Panel collapsed hideTopbar ON Phone ≤ 860 px Popover anchor Row cost
A · rail glyph Works — the rail is the re-expand path Works — the rail is not the topbar Drawer covers the rail; needs the drawer closed App-level layer: rail and panel both clip 36 px of rail; no name on screen
B · tab chip Works — tab row is independent Works — tab row is independent No host: #tab-bar is hidden #editor-pane (bar clips) ≤ 150 px of tab row; name on screen
C · topbar chip Works — topbar is outside the panel No host: topbar is display:none Works — phone forces the topbar; brand hidden In flow, under the chip Zero when hidden; ~120 px of topbar when on

Recommended: B, on the desktop. It is the only host that is alive with the panel collapsed and with hideTopbar on, and it is the only one that names the workspace without a click. It has no phone host, so the phone keeps the shipped drawer bar from workspaces.md — one implementation, two hosts.

Boards draw the panel at 240 px (the design default). config/config.json currently holds sidebarWidth: 581, which only widens the panel; the collapsed and phone conclusions are unchanged.

Not covered here: the shipped bar in #side-panel, the Add/Rename dialog and Settings → Workspaces. They are unchanged by every option and stay as specified in workspaces.md.
